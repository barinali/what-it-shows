The three problems

Parser reads month first. The shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants that write the day first get flipped dates: 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for.

Booking-v3 hint. Tenants on booking-v3 write the day first, but the form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. It is the same flip with a different cause, and it only happens through the form channel. Fixing the parser does not touch it.

One day off. The desk’s own notes list these reports. Their text has no flipped month: a Tuesday shows as a Monday, one client in Lisbon and another in Toronto got different days for the same meeting. That points to time zones at a day boundary, not to date order. It needs an engineer who owns scheduling and time zones.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. Subjects such as “Wrong day on the invite” and “Month and day swapped?” appear in all three problems, so the match could not tell them apart. Intake’s tag was a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it is wrong for part of the queue (see the table). The tenant tells the cause, not the subject and not the browser.

What to do

  • Keep the three tickets apart. Do not merge them back by subject.
  • Ship the parser fix and check the table again: the hint and day-shift rows should not move.
  • Take tickets for the hint and the one-day shift to the teams that own the booking form and scheduling.
